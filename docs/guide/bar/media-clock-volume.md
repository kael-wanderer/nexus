The end of the bar holds a few small rows: the media player, the Trash, minimized windows, Search, the volume and the clock.

## When to use it

- You want to pause a song or skip a track without opening the player.
- You want the time, a calendar, the volume or the Trash on the bar.

## How to use it

### Media player

The media row works with Music, Spotify and VLC. It is off by default and shows only while something plays.

1. Open Settings > Bar and turn on **What is playing**.
2. Play something. The row appears.
3. Click the controls to play, pause, go next or go previous. Drag the timeline to move through the track.

The first time Nexus controls a player, macOS asks for Automation permission for that player. If you refuse, that player cannot be controlled.

In Settings > Appearance, **Media player** is **Wide** or **Compact**. **Wide player shows** is **Progress bar** or **What is playing**. A bar on the left or right edge is compact unless it widens on hover, because a narrow timeline is too short to drag. On a compact row, rest the pointer on it to open a small panel with the controls.

### Clock and calendar

The clock shows the time over the date. Click it to open a month grid. It is on by default. Turn it off with **Clock** in Settings > Bar. The format follows your Mac's region settings.

### Volume

Click the volume row to open a slider. Right-click it for **Mute** or **Unmute**. It is on by default. Turn it off with **Volume** in Settings > Bar. A sound device with no volume control shows no volume row.

### Trash

The Trash row is always there and shows full or empty.

1. Click it to open the Trash in Finder.
2. Right-click it for **Open Trash** or **Empty Trash…**.
3. **Empty Trash…** asks first: "Empty the Trash?" The items are then deleted permanently. Choose **Empty Trash** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

Emptying needs Automation permission for Finder, which macOS asks for the first time.

### Search row

The Search row opens the palette. In Settings > Search, **In the bar, show** is **An icon**, **A search box** or **Nothing**. The shortcut works whichever you pick. See [Search](../search/search.md).

## What it will not do

- The media row does not appear for players other than Music, Spotify and VLC.
- Typing does not happen on the bar. The search box opens the palette.
- Emptying the Trash cannot be undone.
