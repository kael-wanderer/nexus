---
title: Control the bar from the keyboard
summary: Control-Option-Space puts the keyboard on the bar; arrows walk it, Return opens.
---

Keyboard mode lets you move along the bar and open things without the mouse. A ring marks the row the keyboard is on.

## When to use it
- You prefer not to leave the keyboard.
- You want to reach a pinned app, group or folder without searching.

## How to use it
### Walk the bar
1. Press <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>Space</kbd>. The bar shows a ring on the first row, and appears if it was hidden.
2. Move with the arrow keys. <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>←</kbd> go back, <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> go forward, on a bar at any edge.
3. <kbd>Home</kbd> jumps to the first row and <kbd>End</kbd> to the last.
4. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> (or <kbd>Space</kbd>) to open what is focused.
5. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to give the keyboard back. Pressing <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>Space</kbd> again does the same.

If you do nothing for ten seconds, the keyboard goes back by itself.

### Change or turn off the shortcut
1. Open Settings ▸ **Shortcuts**.
2. Switch **Keyboard access to the bar** on or off.
3. To change the keys, click the box under **Focus the bar**, then press the new combination. <kbd>⌫</kbd> resets it to <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>Space</kbd>, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

> [!NOTE]
> <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>F3</kbd> is the macOS shortcut for the Dock. macOS keeps it, so it never reaches Nexus. That is why Nexus uses <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>Space</kbd>.

## What it will not do
- It does not work while the bar is hidden by a full-screen app on that display.
- Other keys still go to the app that had them; Nexus only takes the keys listed above while the ring is showing.
